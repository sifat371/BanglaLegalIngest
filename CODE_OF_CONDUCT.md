# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Contributors and maintainers are expected to keep project spaces respectful, constructive, and
focused on improving the software.

Harassment, discrimination, personal attacks, threats, doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not
acceptable.

## Expected behavior

- discuss code and evidence rather than attacking people;
- give actionable technical feedback;
- respect different levels of experience and language fluency;
- protect private or sensitive information;
- correct mistakes transparently;
- avoid presenting unsupported legal or benchmark claims as established facts.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove contributions that violate these expectations and may restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project community.

For sensitive conduct concerns, use a private GitHub contact/reporting mechanism when available
rather than posting personal information publicly.
